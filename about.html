Who We Are
A diagnostics centre built on precision, empathy, and a genuine commitment to your health.
How ZAKOM Began
ZAKOM Medical Diagnostics was founded in February 2026 by Israel and Kofoworola Balogun, a vision born from a simple belief: that every patient deserves fast, accurate and compassionate diagnostic care.

From day one, we set out to build a centre that combines cutting-edge technology with a deeply human approach to healthcare.
Vision & Mission
Our Vision
To be the most trusted partner in healthcare, setting the regional benchmark for diagnostic excellence and precision.
Our Mission
To deliver accurate, timely diagnostic solutions through cutting-edge technology and expert professionals, upholding the highest standards of care.
What Drives Us
Precision
We deliver accurate, reliable results that physicians and patients can trust completely.
Empathy

Innovation

Teamwork

Trusted & Accredited
