Our Diagnostic Services
From routine lab tests to advanced imaging explore our full range of diagnostic services.
Care That Covers All the Bases
Medical Laboratory Services Comprehensive blood tests, urinalysis, and routine screenings carried out with precision and speed. Our lab is equipped to handle a wide range of diagnostic requests, delivering results you and your physician can confidently act on.
Hormonal Assay Accurate measurement of hormone levels to assess thyroid function, reproductive health, adrenal activity, and more. Essential for diagnosing and monitoring a range of hormonal conditions.
Sonography (Ultrasound Scans) High-resolution ultrasound imaging for abdominal, pelvic, obstetric, and soft tissue assessments. Safe, non-invasive, and performed by trained sonographers.
Digital X-Rays Fast, high-quality digital X-ray imaging for bone, chest, and organ assessment. Lower radiation exposure with clearer, more detailed results than traditional X-ray methods.
Mammography Dedicated breast imaging for early detection of abnormalities and cancer screening. A critical service for women's health, delivered with care and sensitivity.
ECG (Electrocardiogram) Non-invasive heart monitoring to detect irregularities in heart rhythm and electrical activity. Quick, painless, and vital for cardiovascular health assessment.
Echo (Echocardiography) Ultrasound imaging of the heart to evaluate structure, function, and blood flow. Used to diagnose and monitor a range of cardiac conditions.
General Body Checkup A comprehensive head-to-toe health assessment covering key vitals and diagnostic markers. Ideal for routine wellness monitoring or pre-employment medicals.
General Diagnostics Consultation Not sure where to start? Our diagnostic consultation helps you identify the right tests based on your symptoms, history, and health goals.
